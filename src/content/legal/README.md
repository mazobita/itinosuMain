# Legal text

`terms.json` and `privacy.json` are copies of the iOS app's `Itinosu/Resources/Legal/{terms,privacy}.json` (the text
shown at sign-up and from the paywall). The website must show exactly the app's version: after any change in the app
repo, copy both files here again and redeploy. Pages: `/terms`, `/privacy` (src/components/LegalPage.astro).
